# Mistral AI Logo & Brand Guidelines

This document synthesizes the official **Mistral Logo Guidelines** from [`mistral-logo-guidelines.pdf`](file:///c:/Users/Nagarro/Downloads/mistral-studio-marketing/official-designs-and-docs/brand/guidelines/mistral-logo-guidelines.pdf) (2026 Edition). All marketing collateral, design assets, website UI, pitch decks, and field enablement materials produced for **Mistral Studio** must strictly comply with these rules.

---

## 📏 1. Clearspace

To maintain high visual impact and legibility, a mandatory minimum clearspace must surround the logo at all times.

- **Clearspace Unit**: The height of the pixelated 'M' symbol is used as the standard unit of measurement.
- **Application**: 
  - For the **Primary Lockup** (Symbol + Wordmark), maintain a minimum empty boundary equal to the symbol height on all four sides.
  - The same 1-unit clearspace rule applies when the **Symbol** or **Wordmark** is used in isolation.
  - No text, graphic elements, borders, or container edges may enter this designated clearspace zone.

---

## 📐 2. Minimum Sizing

Minimum sizing constraints prevent pixelation, loss of detail, or legibility compromise across digital and print media:

| Element | Minimum Recommended Width |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Lockup** (Symbol + Wordmark) | **100 px** |
| **Symbol in Isolation** ('M' Icon) | **20 px** |

*Note: Below these threshold dimensions, pixel structure and text legibility become compromised.*

---

## 🎨 3. Color Executions & Restraints

### 3.1 Sunset Gradient (Primary Lockup & Icon)
- **Default Expression**: The signature **Sunset Gradient** (warm yellow-orange to deep red gradient) is the primary marker of the Mistral brand.
- **Usage**: Use as the default expression for the symbol across standard **white / light** backgrounds and solid **dark** backgrounds.
- **Contrast**: Engineered to deliver high color contrast and vibrancy without sacrificing accessibility.

### 3.2 Monochrome Executions (Colored & Photo Backgrounds)
- **Complex / Photo Backgrounds**: On photography, busy graphics, or colored background surfaces, the symbol MUST shift to a solid **monochrome** execution.
- **Variants**: Rendered entirely in solid **Black** (`#151524`) or solid **White**, selected based on contrast requirements.

### 3.3 Wordmark Color Restraint
- **Strict Rule**: The "Mistral" wordmark **must never appear in color** (e.g., no orange, yellow, red, or custom gradient text fills).
- **Allowed Colors**: Defaults strictly to solid **Black** (`#151524`) on light backgrounds, or solid **White** on dark/colored backgrounds when accessibility requires.

---

## 🚫 4. Strict Misuses & Don'ts

To preserve brand integrity, the following logo treatments are strictly prohibited across all marketing and product assets:

1. **No Gradient on Photos/Colors**: Do NOT use the sunset gradient version of the logo or symbol over photography, complex graphics, or colored backgrounds.
2. **No Low Contrast**: Do NOT place the logo on low-contrast backgrounds where legibility is compromised.
3. **No Unofficial Variations**: Do NOT use recreated, modified, or unofficial logo lockups (e.g., appending "AI" or "_" to the wordmark, or altering the pixel geometry).
4. **No Wordmark Recoloring**: Do NOT recolor or apply gradients/colors to the wordmark other than solid Black (`#151524`) or White.
5. **No Distortion**: Do NOT skew, stretch, rotate, perspective-shift, or distort the logo or symbol in any way.
6. **No Visual Effects**: Do NOT add drop shadows, outer glows, bevels, outlines, or ambient lighting effects to the logo lockup or symbol.

# Mistral AI Financial Services Industry (FSI) Field Playbook

**Owner:** Senior Product Marketing Manager, Industries (Financial Services)  
**Audience:** Account Executives, Forward Deployed Engineers, Solution Architects, Customer Success, Partner Teams

---

## 1. Executive Field Thesis

### Positioning

> **Controlled AI for Financial Intelligence and Regulated Workflows.**

Mistral's strongest FSI position is not “AI for banks” broadly. It is the combination of:

1. **Deployment control** — self-hosted, private, managed-cloud and data-platform routes depending on the workload.
2. **Model portability and customization** — flexibility to fit into existing enterprise AI estates rather than force platform replacement.
3. **Financial document intelligence** — extraction, comparison and source-grounded reasoning across complex financial documents.
4. **Enterprise controls** — identity, policy, auditability, evidence and human review for sensitive workflows.
5. **Multi-model coexistence** — Mistral can complement Microsoft, AWS, Google, Snowflake and internal AI platforms.
6. **Co-development** — strategic FSI relationships show value in close technical collaboration, not only API access.

### Field priority

| Priority | Play | Primary value |
|---|---|---|
| **P0 Anchor** | **Controlled AI for Regulated Financial Workflows** | Apply AI to sensitive workflows while preserving the institution's required control model |
| **P0 Technical Wedge** | **Financial Intelligence & Document Automation** | Extract, compare, reason over and cite complex financial documents |
| **P1 Expansion** | **Developer & IT AI** | Extend controlled AI into code, IT and developer workflows |
| Horizontal | **Employee AI** | Land / expand adoption; not the main differentiated FSI thesis |

### What good looks like

Every opportunity should answer five questions:

> **Workload -> Buyer -> Control requirement -> Proof -> Next decision**

If those five are unclear, the opportunity is not field-ready.

## 2. Evidence Rules Sellers Must Follow

Use these rules in every customer conversation:

- Relationship != investment != partnership != deployment != production.
- Strategic partnership != mature production proof.
- ISV-embedded usage != direct Mistral procurement.
- Technology availability != customer usage.
- Regulation != proof that regulation caused the purchase.
- On-premises != automatically air-gapped.
- Product requirement != current Product capability.
- Customer-wide AI scale != Mistral-specific scale unless the source says so.

**Field principle:** be specific enough to be credible, not broad enough to be disproven.

## 3. Play 1 — Controlled AI for Regulated Financial Workflows

### Buyer problem

Financial institutions want AI in high-sensitivity workflows without losing control over data, access, model choice, evidence or human accountability.

### Best-fit workflows

- KYC / onboarding document review
- AML / financial-crime investigation support
- credit and lending document analysis
- policy / contract review
- fraud-investigation support
- regulated knowledge workflows
- controlled analyst or employee assistants for sensitive domains

### ICP signals

Prioritize accounts where several are true:

- sensitive customer, transaction, credit or investment data;
- strong confidentiality, residency or third-party-risk requirements;
- private-cloud / self-hosted / controlled AI strategy;
- internal multi-model platform;
- repeated document-review workload;
- material Security / Model Risk / Legal review before production;
- need to evaluate or adapt models on proprietary data.

### Buying committee

**Executive:** CRO, CCO, CIO, CISO  
**Champion:** Financial Crime, Credit / Underwriting, Compliance Transformation, Operations  
**Technical:** AI Engineering, Enterprise Architecture, Security Architecture, Data / ML Platform  
**Control functions:** Model Risk, Privacy, Legal, Internal Audit, Procurement

### Discovery

1. Which AI workflows are blocked or delayed by Security, Model Risk, Legal, residency or audit requirements?
2. Which workflows create the largest manual review burden or longest turnaround time?
3. What evidence must control functions see before approving production?
4. Which decisions must remain human-controlled?
5. Does the institution already operate a multi-model gateway?
6. What representative data can be used for evaluation?
7. What production or commercial decision follows if the evaluation succeeds?

### Proof anchors

- **BNP Paribas:** strongest proof for large-bank multi-model adoption, co-development, document / analysis workflows and progressive enterprise expansion.
- **HSBC:** strongest global-bank proof for self-hosted Mistral deployment, document-heavy lending / financing work, multilingual, employee and developer workflows.
- **La Banque Postale:** strongest bank-infrastructure / on-premises proof; 5,000-employee initial rollout plus IT and business workflows.
- **ABN AMRO:** strategic partnership proof for European autonomy, resilience, security, privacy and co-development; production maturity remains early / unclear.
- **Groupe Mutuel:** strategic insurance / protection reference; do not generalize beyond explicitly named workflows.

### Evaluation motion

**Input:** customer-approved historical or synthetic cases.  
**Workflow:** case / document -> contextual evidence -> model-supported analysis -> human disposition -> traceable record.  
**Measure:** factual support, source traceability, analyst usefulness, review ergonomics and integration feasibility.  
**Decision:** proceed, re-scope, stop, or identify a Product / governance blocker.

**CTA:**

> **Run a controlled architecture and workload evaluation on one real financial workflow with Security / Model Risk success criteria agreed up front.**

## 4. Play 2 — Financial Intelligence & Document Automation

### Buyer problem

Analysts, lenders and deal teams spend substantial time extracting, comparing and synthesizing information across complex documents. The winning motion is **traceable document intelligence on the customer's own data**, not generic benchmark superiority.

### Priority motions

**Financial Document Intelligence**
- credit files and lending packages
- financial statements, tables and footnotes
- policies, contracts and regulatory filings
- research / knowledge corpora

**Due Diligence & Deal Analysis**
- data-room review
- investment / credit memo support
- cross-document comparison
- financial and legal issue identification

**Research / Credit / Investment Intelligence**
- source-grounded synthesis
- enterprise knowledge search
- structured comparison across filings, research and internal material

### Buying committee

**Executive:** CIO, Head of Investment, Head of Credit, COO  
**Champion:** Deal Technology, Research, Operational Due Diligence, Credit Transformation  
**Technical:** AI / Data Platform, Document AI, Knowledge Platform  
**Blockers:** Information Security, Legal / confidentiality, Model Risk, Procurement

### Qualification

Prioritize when:

- document volume or complexity materially affects turnaround or coverage;
- the team can provide a representative golden set;
- source-level citations or extraction fidelity matter;
- confidentiality limits use of public tools;
- a named business owner will judge usefulness;
- Mistral can coexist with the existing AI stack.

### Proof anchors

- **Ardian GAIA:** strongest private-markets proof for document summarization, analysis, comparison, multi-model coexistence and measurable adoption.
- **HSBC:** supports document-heavy lending / financing analysis and multilingual reasoning.
- **BNP Paribas:** supports internal document search, data extraction, complex financial analysis and enterprise multi-model adoption.

### Customer-specific evaluation

Measure on a customer-approved golden set:

- field-level extraction precision / recall;
- table-structure fidelity;
- citation / source-location accuracy;
- factual consistency on financial figures;
- cross-document comparison quality;
- latency / throughput;
- analyst acceptance / time-to-decision;
- human-review rate.

**CTA:**

> **Benchmark one representative deal or credit-document set against the customer's current process using agreed accuracy, traceability and decision criteria.**

## 5. Play 3 — Developer & IT AI (P1 Expansion)

Use developer AI primarily as an **expansion wedge** inside existing accounts.

### Relevant needs

- code assistance on sensitive repositories;
- code search / embedding;
- documentation and test support;
- modernization / refactoring experiments;
- private developer-assistant deployment.

**Proof:** HSBC and La Banque Postale support developer / IT use cases. Do not claim customer-proven COBOL-to-Java outcomes without exact evidence.

**CTA:**

> **Run a developer evaluation on a representative internal codebase with customer-defined quality, security and productivity measures.**

## 6. Employee AI — Land / Expand, Not the Industry Thesis

Employee-facing AI appears across several lighthouse accounts and can accelerate adoption. Use it to:

- establish organizational familiarity with Mistral;
- expand from specialist teams into wider employee populations;
- generate governance and usage learnings for higher-value controlled workflows.

Do **not** position generic workplace productivity as the main FSI differentiator against Microsoft or Google.

## 7. Competitive Routing

Classify every opportunity before building a battlecard.

| Route | Use when |
|---|---|
| **WIN** | An evidence-backed requirement maps directly to a Mistral advantage |
| **WEDGE** | Mistral can own one strategic workload inside a broader incumbent architecture |
| **COEXIST** | The customer keeps Microsoft / AWS / Google / Snowflake / an internal platform and adds Mistral |
| **PARTNER** | A vertical application or GSI owns the workflow; Mistral provides the model / AI layer |
| **AVOID FORCED COMPETITION** | There is no differentiated workload or the incumbent owns the application layer |

### Competitor guidance

- **Microsoft / Azure:** wedge on portable / self-hosted options and specialized controlled workloads; coexist with Microsoft productivity. Do not claim Microsoft has no local / disconnected AI options broadly.
- **AWS:** coexist through Bedrock / marketplace where useful; wedge on direct model control and portable deployment. Do not claim AWS cannot support private / local architectures.
- **Google Cloud:** coexist where Vertex / GCP is strategic; wedge on portability and avoiding single-platform dependence.
- **OpenAI / Anthropic direct:** wedge where portability, self-hosting or customization matters; avoid replacement when managed-cloud workflows are already working.
- **Internal AI platforms:** position Mistral as the model / deployment / customization layer inside them.
- **Vertical ISVs:** partner when the application owns the workflow.

### Reframe or walk away when

- the requirement is only generic M365 / Workspace productivity;
- a specialized KYC / sanctions / claims platform owns the workflow;
- the customer wants a full consumer-banking application rather than an AI layer;
- there is no control, customization, document-intelligence or multi-model requirement;
- success depends on unsupported benchmark, regulatory or TCO claims.

## 8. Five Deal-Acceleration Mechanisms

| Blocker | Mechanism | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Use-case ambiguity | **Workload Prioritization Worksheet** | ranked workloads by value, feasibility, data readiness, governance and repeatability |
| Security / architecture uncertainty | **Controlled Deployment & Security Blueprint** | agreed deployment options, data flow, control responsibilities and open questions |
| Accuracy / trust uncertainty | **Workload Evaluation Kit** | golden-set benchmark with source traceability |
| POV scope creep | **POV Decision Blueprint** | scope, dataset, success criteria, owners and next decision |
| Procurement / economics uncertainty | **Deployment Economics Model** | customer-specific scenarios with assumptions visible |

A **Financial AI Controls Mapping** can be attached when Legal / Compliance needs a requirement-to-control view. It is not a legal-compliance certification.

## 9. Field-Readiness Gate

A play or field motion is ready to scale only when it has:

1. clear positioning;
2. buyer / champion / blocker map;
3. discovery questions;
4. evidence-backed customer proof;
5. competitive routing guidance;
6. FDE-approved demo / evaluation path;
7. known Product limitations;
8. CTA / next decision;
9. measurement plan.

**Ownership:** AEs own opportunity qualification. FDEs own technical implementation and demo engineering. PMM owns industry positioning, proof packaging, playbooks and enablement.

**Maintenance:** refresh claims, customer classifications, Product capabilities and competitive guidance whenever new evidence changes a field decision.
